Cookie and Browser Storage Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026 · Version: 2026-10-13

The service uses essential browser storage to keep your session available and remember this preference. Authentication uses a server session cookie; a session-storage token is also used as a browser compatibility fallback. The site stores a minimal account identifier/role snapshot and cookie choice in local storage. No analytics or advertising cookies/scripts are currently configured in the customer frontend.

Essential storage is required for sign-in and account actions. “Accept all” is recorded separately, but there are currently no optional analytics or advertising technologies to enable. If these are introduced, this notice must be updated and a valid choice collected before enabling them where required.

You can revisit the banner below or clear site data in your browser. Clearing session data may sign you out.
